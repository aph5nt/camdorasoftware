Where we go deep

The work we get asked back for.

On-chain data at scale

Indexers that keep pace with a live chain. Decoders for transfers, swaps, bridges and smart-account activity. Graph models that let an analyst trace funds across millions of addresses.

  • Full-history EVM indexing, genesis to tip
  • Money-flow graphs for AML and risk screening
  • DEX, lending and bridge decoding

High-throughput data pipelines

Ingest, store and query hundreds of millions of rows a day. Column stores, graph databases and the writers that keep them current without falling behind.

  • StarRocks, ClickHouse, Neo4j, PostgreSQL
  • Batch and streaming writers tuned to the store
  • Backfills that finish, and can be re-run

Distributed system architecture

Kubernetes-native services with clear boundaries, honest failure modes and a scaling plan you can read. Drawn out and agreed before a line of code.

  • Service and data-ownership boundaries
  • Blue-green and A/B serving lanes
  • Observability built in, not bolted on

Quality and testing

Automated suites from the first commit, disposable integration environments, and performance numbers you can put in front of a board.

  • Unit, integration and end-to-end coverage
  • Throughput and load evidence per release
  • Continuous integration gates that mean something

How an engagement runs

Clear steps. No surprises.

  1. 1

    Understand

    Goal, constraints and the numbers that matter. Short calls, written notes, plain language.

  2. 2

    Design

    Architecture and data flow drawn out and agreed. Risks named early, with a cost on each.

  3. 3

    Build and prove

    Small reviewed changes. Tests from day one. Working software you can see every week.

  4. 4

    Run

    Deployment, monitoring and hand-over. We stay on until it is stable in production.

Working with us

Three ways to bring us in.

Project delivery

A defined outcome, a fixed team, a date. We own the design, the build and the proof, and hand over a system your team can run.

Best for: a new product, a platform rebuild, a chain integration.

Embedded engineers

Senior engineers inside your team for a season. Same tools, same stand-ups, same standards. Capacity and experience without a hiring cycle.

Best for: a roadmap that outgrew the team, a hard subsystem nobody owns.

Architecture and review

A second pair of experienced eyes on a design, a codebase or a scaling problem. A written report with options, costs and a recommendation.

Best for: a decision that is expensive to get wrong.

About

Small, senior, and here since 2013.

Camdora Software is a UK engineering company. For over a decade we have built and run software for finance, blockchain and data-heavy businesses, from first design to production.

Every engagement is led by an engineer who has designed and operated production systems, and who writes the code as well as the plan. You talk to the people doing the work.

  • Plain language. Decisions are explained so anyone in the room can make them.
  • Numbers, not adjectives. Throughput, cost and risk stated with figures and sources.
  • Written down. Architecture, trade-offs and runbooks live in the repository, not in someone's head.
  • Proof before promise. A feature is done when the test and the dashboard say so.
